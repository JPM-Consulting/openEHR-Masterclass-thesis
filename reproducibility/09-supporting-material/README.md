# Supporting Material

This directory contains supporting resources for the study **Representing Episode-of-Care Relationships in openEHR: A Comparative Evaluation of Three Implementation Patterns.**

These resources provide educational background and historical context. They are not independent evidence for the functional, architectural, or technical findings reported in the manuscript. The study's evaluation and reproducibility artifacts are documented in directories `01-conceptual-model` through `08-technical-proof-of-concept`.

## Contents

### Tutorials

Directory: [`tutorials/`](tutorials/)

Two educational tutorial sessions introduce Solon's episode-oriented medical record and its modeling in openEHR. They provide background on the conceptual foundations and development of the three implementation patterns examined in the study.

See [`tutorials/README.md`](tutorials/README.md) for details.

### EHRCON26 Conference Material

Directory: [`ehrcon26/`](ehrcon26/)

This directory preserves the poster and five-minute presentation prepared for EHRCON26. These materials reflect an earlier stage of the research and are retained to document its development and conference presentation.

See [`ehrcon26/README.md`](ehrcon26/README.md) for details, including an explanation of the Medical Record Entry count reported in the earlier materials.

## Relationship to Frontiers Supplementary Material

These resources are included in the repository for educational and historical purposes. They should not be treated as Supplementary Material for the Frontiers article unless explicitly identified as such in the final publication.

## Evidence Boundary

The materials in `09-supporting-material` provide background, documentation, and scientific provenance. They do not independently substantiate the comparative findings reported in the manuscript.

The corresponding evidence and reproducibility artifacts are organized in directories `01`–`08`, covering the conceptual model, implementation patterns, synthetic dataset, functional evaluation, architectural evaluation, retrieval examples, manuscript-to-artifact mapping, and technical proof of concept. Each evidence source remains subject to the scope and limitations documented in its respective directory.
